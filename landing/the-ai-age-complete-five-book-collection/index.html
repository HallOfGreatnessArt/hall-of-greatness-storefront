THE AI AGE THE COMPLETE FIVE-BOOK COLLECTION Five essential books. One connected journey through the age of artificial intelligence.

Artificial intelligence is changing how we understand the world, how decisions are made, how power is distributed, how people work, and what societies must protect. To navigate this transformation, we need more than technical explanations or predictions. We need a clear view of the systems, the human questions, and the choices shaping what comes next.

THE AI AGE — THE COMPLETE FIVE-BOOK COLLECTION brings together five complementary books by EMAD ALSHAYEB, published by HALL OF GREATNESS Publishing. Together, they move from understanding AI’s foundations to examining its hidden mechanisms, its implications for humanity, the global structures of technological power, and the future of work.

THE FIVE BOOKS

1. THE AI POWER GAME — UNDERSTAND • ADAPT • THRIVE Your guide to the new era of artificial intelligence. Explore the foundations of AI, its economic and social impact, global competition, business, work, security, trust, responsible use, and practical ways to prepare for change.

2. THE AI BLACK BOX Go beyond the interface to understand how AI systems learn, why their inner workings can be difficult to inspect, how they are evaluated, where they fail, and why transparency, evidence, accountability, and human oversight matter.

3. THE HUMANITY TEST Can humanity remain human in the age of superintelligence? Examine dignity, identity, consciousness, empathy, relationships, privacy, freedom, human agency, and the values that should guide technological progress.

4. THE ALGORITHM EMPIRE Who controls the intelligence that increasingly shapes our world? Investigate data, chips, computing infrastructure, platforms, energy, surveillance, cybersecurity, digital sovereignty, geopolitics, and the rules governing technological power.

5. THE LAST JOB How will AI transform work—and what must humans do next? Explore changing tasks and occupations, productivity, skills, AI coworkers and agents, workplace monitoring, worker voice, fair employment, and the choices required for a human-centred transition.

WHY READ THE COLLECTION? See the AI transformation from five connected perspectives: foundations, systems, humanity, power, and work. Build a balanced understanding beyond hype and fear. Connect technical developments with economic, social, ethical, and institutional consequences. Keep human judgment, dignity, responsibility, and agency at the centre of progress.

WHO IS THIS COLLECTION FOR? Students, professionals, educators, entrepreneurs, business leaders, technology practitioners, policymakers, and curious readers who want to understand artificial intelligence and its consequences without needing to be technical specialists.

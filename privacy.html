Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, how this gallery website is intended to work. It is an informational storefront that presents digital artworks and links visitors to Gumroad for product details, checkout, and file delivery.

Information on this gallery

The gallery does not ask visitors to create an account or submit payment details. Search and category filters operate in your browser. The website may receive standard technical information through its hosting provider, such as request and security logs, as part of delivering and protecting the site.

Third-party services

When you follow a product or store link, you leave this gallery and visit Gumroad or another linked service. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review the relevant provider's information before submitting personal or payment details. This gallery does not process payments or deliver purchased files.

Cookies and analytics

This gallery does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. A third-party service you choose to visit may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.

Contact

For questions about this gallery or its artworks, use the creator's Gumroad storefront contact options.
